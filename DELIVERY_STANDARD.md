# Delivery Standard

CrossingKey engagements should be scoped around an observable outcome rather than vague access to technical labor.

A delivery package should identify:

1. objective;
2. in-scope systems;
3. required access;
4. implementation boundary;
5. acceptance evidence;
6. handoff material;
7. excluded work;
8. next options.

No engagement is represented as completed until the agreed deliverable exists and the relevant verification step has been performed.
